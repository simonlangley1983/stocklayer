From a list to an answer

StockLayer is designed to make exploring the market quick and intuitive.

  1. STEP 01

    Choose what matters

    Start with the measure you want to explore, from FTSE rank and market value to share-price performance and StockLayer’s Growth Confidence score.

  2. STEP 02

    Set the timeframe

    For performance measures, choose the period you want to examine, from recent movements to longer-term trends.

  3. STEP 03

    Choose how to measure change

    View performance as a percentage or, where appropriate, as an absolute monetary change.

  4. STEP 04

    Compare

    Sort the company list by any supported measure to quickly see the leaders, laggards and everything in between.

Understanding the numbers

StockLayer uses familiar market measures alongside a small number of calculated indicators. Here’s what each one tells you.

FTSE rank
A company’s position when FTSE 100 constituents are ranked by market value. Rank 1 represents the largest company.
Share price
The market price of a single share. Useful for tracking price movements, but not for comparing the overall size of different companies.
Share price change
How much the share price has risen or fallen over your selected period. Available as an absolute value or percentage.
Market value
Also known as market capitalisation. The total market value of a company’s outstanding shares and a useful measure of company size.
Market value change
The increase or decrease in a company’s total market value over the selected period.
StockLayer indicatorGrowth Confidence
StockLayer’s proprietary 0–100 indicator designed to provide a consistent view of potential future growth using company and market data.

Absolute change vs percentage change

The same price movement can be expressed in two ways. StockLayer lets you switch between them depending on what you want to compare.

+
+£2.00Absolute change
=
+20.0%Percentage change
→
£12.00New price

Percentage change is generally more useful when comparing companies with very different share prices. Absolute change shows the actual movement in pounds and pence.

Built for research, not recommendations

StockLayer is designed to make public company information easier to explore and compare. It does not provide investment advice, recommendations or predictions and should not be used as the sole basis for an investment decision.
