The short version

StockLayer does not sell your personal information. It does not currently use advertising cookies, behavioural tracking or analytics cookies. Essential browser storage is used only to remember choices you make on the site.

If that changes, this page and the cookie controls will be updated before any optional technology is switched on.

Who is responsible for your information?

StockLayer is operated by Simon Langley, who is responsible for deciding how personal information is used for this site.

For privacy questions, contact Simon at simon@simonlangley.co.uk.

Information we use

When you browse

The web host and security services may receive technical details needed to send the site to your device and keep it secure. These can include your IP address, browser type, requested page, time of request and basic error or security logs.

When you contact StockLayer

If you contact Simon, the information you choose to provide will be used to read and reply to your message. This may include your name, contact details and the contents of your message.

Accounts

StockLayer does not currently offer account registration, direct messaging or Google sign-in. If this changes, this notice will be updated before personal information is collected for those features.

Legal reasons for using information

  • Legitimate interests: to operate, secure and improve StockLayer, and to respond to messages, provided those interests do not override your rights.
  • Legal obligations: where information must be used or retained to comply with the law.

Cookies, browser storage and PECR

One of my roles at Barclays was Head of Digital Privacy, so obviously I want to ensure that StockLayer stays on the right side of UK digital privacy regulations, which I came to love and admire so much.

This cookie statement is dedicated to my good pal Campbell Cowie. Colleagues at Barclays used to call us the Ant and Dec of Digital Privacy. Actually, that's a lie - we just called ourselves that.

What StockLayer stores

PECR covers cookies and other ways of storing or reading information on your device, including local browser storage. StockLayer currently uses only the items below. They remember choices that you actively make and are not used to track you across websites.

This approach follows the ICO's current guidance on storage and access exceptions. The preference cookie remembers the privacy choice you request. The introductory panel setting changes the site to match your chosen appearance and can be reversed on the companies page.

No optional cookies are active

There are currently no advertising, profiling or analytics cookies on StockLayer. Optional technologies must not be added without updating this policy and asking for valid consent first.

Other services used by the site

StockLayer does not sell personal information. The site currently connects to a small number of other services to provide its content:

  • Web hosting and security providers deliver the site and may process technical request logs.
  • GitHub supplies public company data files used by the site. A request to GitHub can include your IP address and browser details.
  • Logo.dev may supply company logos on company detail pages. A logo request can include your IP address, browser details and the company domain being requested.
  • External links such as LinkedIn open another organisation's website. Its own privacy policy applies after you follow the link.

If the services used by StockLayer or the way information is shared changes, this notice will be updated before the new processing begins.

How long is information kept?

Information should be kept only for as long as it is needed. Browser preferences last for the periods shown above. Messages are kept only while they are needed to respond and manage any reasonable follow-up. Technical logs follow the retention settings of the final hosting and security providers.

StockLayer does not currently operate account registration, contact forms or analytics. If any of these features are introduced, this notice will be updated before the feature starts collecting personal information.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the situation, UK data protection law may give you the right to:

  • ask for a copy of your personal information;
  • ask for incorrect information to be corrected;
  • ask for information to be deleted or its use restricted;
  • object to certain uses of your information;
  • receive information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent at any time where consent is being used.

These rights are not absolute in every situation. You will not be charged for making a normal request.

Questions, requests and complaints

To ask a privacy question or exercise a data protection right, email Simon at simon@simonlangley.co.uk.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. It is helpful to contact StockLayer first so there is an opportunity to put things right.

This policy will be reviewed when the site's features, providers or use of personal information changes.
