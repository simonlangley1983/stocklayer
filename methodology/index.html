How each figure is calculated

The calculations are simple. Here is what StockLayer does.

FTSE rank

Largest market value comes first

We put FTSE 100 companies in order using their current market value. The company with the largest value is number 1.

Share price change

New price compared with old price

We compare today's share price with the share price at the start of the time you chose.

£10 becomes £12 = +£2 or +20%
Market value change

New value compared with old value

We compare the company's current market value with its market value at the start of the time you chose.

£1 billion becomes £1.2 billion = +£200 million or +20%
Global market cap

A company's place in the world

We compare the company's market value with companies around the world. A smaller rank number means a higher place.

How Growth Confidence is built

Growth Confidence is a StockLayer score from 0 to 100. It joins four signals together.

35%

Share price movement

We look at recent share price changes over different lengths of time.

30%

Company reports

We look for useful words and themes in company reports when this information is available.

20%

Market position

We look at the company's size and its global market value rank.

15%

Risk balance

We look at risk, price movement and how much the price moves up and down.

StockLayer blends these four signals and keeps the final score between 0 and 100. A higher score means the signals look stronger. It does not mean growth is certain.

What you should know

  • Prices and company values can change at any time.
  • Some information may arrive later than other information.
  • Older company figures may not always be available.
  • Automated analysis can miss details or make mistakes.
  • A score is a research tool. It is not a promise or financial advice.
