2006 – 2025

Nearly 20 years at Barclays

After university I joined Barclays and spent nearly two decades working across websites, Online Banking, mobile and digital products. It gave me a deep appreciation for how people use technology day to day and how difficult it can be to make sophisticated systems feel simple.

Working at scale with millions of customers, I saw first hand the importance of clear data, well designed tools and the value of looking beyond the surface.

2024 – Present

Splunk, now part of Cisco

I moved to Splunk as a Customer Success Executive, which shortly afterwards became part of Cisco. It has been a great opportunity to work with large organisations across a range of industries, helping them get more value from their data.

It also reinforced my interest in how businesses actually operate, the decisions they make and the factors that drive their performance over time.

2026

That idea became StockLayer

One of my projects was a history of Cisco’s stock going back to its IPO in 1990. I wanted to understand the company beyond today’s share price, looking at performance alongside acquisitions, leadership, major events and the wider story.

That work got me wondering whether the same approach could make it easier to explore UK listed companies and understand the stories behind their share prices.

💡

Why the UK? Why now?

There is huge depth and opportunity in UK companies. StockLayer is a useful, free resource for anyone who wants to learn more, explore ideas and do their own research.
