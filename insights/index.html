How to use an insight

An insight is a clue. It is not the whole story.

1

Notice the change

Look for a company or number that stands out from the others.

2

Ask why

Open the company page and look for news, reports and business changes.

3

Check more facts

Use other trusted sources before you make any decision.

A simple example

One number is more useful when you compare it with another number.

+25% Company share price
compared with
+10% FTSE 100 average

The company rose by more than the FTSE 100 average. The next step is to find out why.

Insights are not advice

StockLayer shows patterns and summaries to help with research. It does not know what will happen next and it does not tell you what to buy or sell.
